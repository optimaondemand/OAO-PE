Why Move Your Body?

Quick quiz: Have you ever played tag? Danced to your favorite song? Ridden a bike, or chased a friend across the playground?

BOOM 💥 — that was physical activity! Physical activity is any time you move your body in a way that gets your heart pumping and your muscles working. And here's the cool part: you don't have to be an athlete to do it. Every single human on Earth needs to move every day. That includes YOU. 🎉

💡 Did You Know?

Your heart beats about 100,000 times every single day — even when you're sleeping! When you exercise, it beats even faster to get extra oxygen to your muscles. Try this: put your hand on your chest right now. Feel that bump-bump? That's your heart saying hi! 💓

Station 3

Moving = Happy Feelings

Here's a secret most kids don't know: moving your body changes how you FEEL on the inside too.

When you're active, your brain makes special chemicals called endorphins (en-DOR-fins) — they're like tiny happiness sparkles for your mind. ✨ Moving can make you feel:

  • 😄 Happier — like everything is just a little brighter
  • 😌 Calmer — your worries shrink down small
  • 🎯 More focused — your brain is ready to learn at school
  • 😴 Sleepier at bedtime — in a good way!

🌎 Real-World Connection

Real talk: Ever notice how recess makes you feel awesome? Or how a quick dance break wakes you up when you're tired? That's not magic — that's your body and brain working together because you MOVED. 🎮 You can use this power any time you feel grumpy or stuck. Just take 2 minutes to wiggle, hop, or stretch and watch what happens!

Station 4

Movement Builds Friendships

PE isn't just about getting strong — it's also about getting CLOSE with the people around you. When you play games, dance, or do activities with others, you practice:

  • 🤝 Teamwork — figuring out how to win together
  • 📜 Following rules so the game is fair for everyone
  • 😂 Laughing, sharing, and cheering each other on

Being a good teammate is a skill you can practice your whole life — and it starts right here in PE.

💭 Think About It

Think about a time you played with a friend and it felt SUPER fun. What made it fun? Was it the game, the people, the silliness, or all three? 💭
